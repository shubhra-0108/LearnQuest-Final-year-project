# LearnQuest — Social Impact Startup Plan

## 🎯 Executive Summary
LearnQuest is an educational technology social venture addressing the foundational learning crisis in India's low-income and rural communities. By combining **AI-driven adaptive micro-learning** with **guardian-controlled micro-incentives**, LearnQuest motivates children to build daily study habits while relieving financial pressure that forces premature school dropout.

---

## 📊 The Need: ASER 2024 & Census Data
- **Low Reading Fluency**: ASER 2024 revealed that only ~27% of Class 3 children in rural India can read a Class 2 level text.
- **Foundational Numeracy**: Only ~31% of Class 5 students can perform simple division.
- **Dropout Pull**: ~8% of 15–16 year olds are out of formal education, driven by informal labor demands.
- **Child Labor Baseline**: Census data records over 10 million working children aged 5–14.

---

## 💡 The Solution
1. **Adaptive Quizzing**: Short 5–10 minute quizzes calibrated by Bayesian Knowledge Tracing so the child experiences a ~70% success rate (challenging without frustration).
2. **Effort Micro-Incentives**: Rewarding steady participation, effort on wrong answers, and skill improvement. 10 points = ₹1; capped at ₹10/day.
3. **Parental Authority**: Money is disbursed exclusively to the verified parent or guardian's UPI account with explicit parental approval per transaction.
4. **Zero Ads, Zero Distractions**: High-dopamine game mechanics focused strictly on literacy and numeracy.

### Sustainability
- **B2B / B2NGO Licensing**: NGOs, CSR foundations, and state education departments sponsor student cohorts.
- **Corporate CSR Mandate**: 2% CSR compliance funding directed to verifiable learning outcomes.

---

## 🗺️ Roadmap & Milestones
- **Phase 1 (Pilot)**: 30–50 child pilot in partnership with a local community learning center.
- **Phase 2 (Content Scale)**: Expand question bank to 2,000+ teacher-reviewed items across Classes 1–8.
- **Phase 3 (Offline Mode)**: Progressive Web App (PWA) with local IndexedDB storage and opportunistic sync for low-connectivity rural belts.
